About this oil

Our white sage oil comes from the same still runs as our White Sage Hydrosol: the oil is the small layer that floats on top of the water. White sage oil is fresh, herbal and resinous, led by 1,8-cineole with camphor and pinene, and it carries the clear, grounding character of the dried leaf without any smoke. A little goes a long way: a few drops in a diffuser scent a whole room. White sage holds deep cultural and ceremonial meaning for Indigenous peoples of California, and wild stands have suffered from over-harvesting. Every drop of ours is distilled from garden-grown plants, never wild-harvested.

How much to use
  • Face: 3 drops per tablespoon of carrier oil (1%)
  • Body: 6 drops per tablespoon of carrier oil (2%)
  • Diffuser: 3–5 drops in the water

Never use it undiluted on skin. Rich in 1,8-cineole and camphor: avoid during pregnancy or breastfeeding, if you have epilepsy, and near the faces of children under 10.

Ingredients

100% pure essential oil. No fillers, no carrier oil, no synthetic fragrance.

Care

Keep the cap tight and the bottle out of heat and direct sunlight. Keep out of reach of children and pets. Essential oils are flammable: keep away from open flame.

What is an essential oil?

An essential oil is the concentrated aromatic oil a plant gives off in a steam still. It floats on top of the hydrosol and is collected separately. It is many times stronger than a hydrosol, so dilute it in a carrier oil before it goes on skin (see How much to use), and never take it by mouth.
