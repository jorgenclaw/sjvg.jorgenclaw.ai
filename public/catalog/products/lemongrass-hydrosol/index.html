Lemongrass Hydrosol

Cymbopogon citratus

Description

Steam-distilled from lemongrass leaf and stalk, this hydrosol carries a bright, grassy bouquet built on citral — the same aromatic aldehyde that gives lemon peel its sharp citrus top note. Lemongrass hydrosol makes a toning facial mist well suited to oily or blemish-prone skin, a refreshing addition to hair rinses, and a natural water substitute in lotions and clay masks. Popular as a base for body and room sprays, particularly blended with a few drops of citronella, lemongrass, or eucalyptus essential oil for an outdoor/insect-deterring variation. Also a bright note in DIY household cleaners.

Technical Note

Distilled from fresh lemongrass stalk and leaf via steam distillation. As with all hydrosols, natural batch variation in scent and color should be expected. Intended for cosmetic and household use only.

Ingredients

100% pure hydrosol. No fillers, no synthetic fragrance, no preservatives added.
