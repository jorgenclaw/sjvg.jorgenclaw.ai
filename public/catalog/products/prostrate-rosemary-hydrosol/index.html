Prostrate Rosemary Hydrosol

Salvia rosmarinus 'Prostratus' (syn. Rosmarinus officinalis 'Prostratus')

Description

Steam-distilled from prostrate rosemary leaf, this hydrosol carries a woody, resinous bouquet built on 1,8-cineole and camphor, with a bright, pine-like top note from alpha-pinene. Trailing rosemary tends to run a touch milder and less camphorous than upright culinary rosemary, giving a rounder, more wearable version of that classic evergreen scent. A brisk, invigorating facial and scalp mist, a natural water substitute in lotions and hair rinses, or a bracing addition to a morning routine. Popular in DIY hair care for the scalp, and a clean, herbaceous note in room and linen sprays.

Technical Note

Distilled from fresh prostrate rosemary leaf and stem via steam distillation. As with all hydrosols, natural batch variation in scent and color should be expected. Intended for cosmetic and household use only.

Ingredients

100% pure hydrosol. No fillers, no synthetic fragrance, no preservatives added.
