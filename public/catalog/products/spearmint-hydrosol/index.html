About this hydrosol

Steam-distilled from spearmint leaf, this hydrosol carries the sweet, familiar bouquet of spearmint, built on carvone with limonene and 1,8-cineole in support. Spearmint holds little menthol, so it reads softer and sweeter than peppermint, without the sharp cooling edge. Makes a soothing facial mist for warm days or after sun exposure, a cooling toner, a water substitute in lotions and after-sun gels, or a light room and pillow spray. Pairs well with our lavender, lemongrass, or Meyer lemon hydrosols.

Technical note

Distilled from fresh spearmint leaf and stem via steam distillation. As with all hydrosols, natural batch variation in scent and color should be expected. Intended for cosmetic and household use only.

Ingredients

100% pure hydrosol. No fillers, no synthetic fragrance, no preservatives added.

Care

Shake well. Keep out of direct sunlight. Refrigeration extends shelf life, but it isn't required.

What is a hydrosol?

A hydrosol is the fragrant water that comes out of a steam still along with a plant's essential oil. It carries the plant's water-soluble aromatic compounds at a much gentler strength than the oil, so it can go straight onto skin, linens, or into recipes without diluting.
