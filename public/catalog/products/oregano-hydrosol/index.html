Oregano Hydrosol

Origanum vulgare

Description

Steam-distilled from oregano leaf, this hydrosol carries oregano's signature phenolic bouquet: carvacrol and thymol forward, warm, savory, and edging toward medicinal. Naturally antimicrobial and antifungal in character, it's prized as a purifying mist for the skin and scalp, a natural addition to DIY surface cleaners, and a traditional gargle or steam-inhalation herb for seasonal wellness. Its aromatic compounds carry real bite even in hydrosol form — more so than most hydrosols — so a patch test is recommended before facial use, especially for sensitive skin.

Technical Note

Distilled from fresh oregano leaf and flower via steam distillation. Naturally rich in carvacrol and other phenolic compounds. As with all hydrosols, natural batch variation in scent and strength should be expected. Intended for cosmetic and household use only; not a substitute for medical treatment.

Ingredients

100% pure hydrosol. No fillers, no synthetic fragrance, no preservatives added.
