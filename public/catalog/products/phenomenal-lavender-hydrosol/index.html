Phenomenal Lavender Hydrosol

Lavandula × intermedia 'Phenomenal'

Description

Steam-distilled from our own 'Phenomenal' lavandin flower spikes, this hydrosol carries the classic floral-herbaceous bouquet of lavender — linalool and linalyl acetate forward — with a touch more camphor than true English lavender. 'Phenomenal' is a robust lavandin cultivar prized by growers for its vigor, disease resistance, and unusually high oil content, traits that carry through into a hydrosol with real depth and staying power. A go-to calming facial and body mist, a natural addition to linen and pillow sprays, or a water substitute in lotions, creams, and bath products. Also a favorite for cooling sunburned or irritated skin.

Technical Note

Distilled from fresh 'Phenomenal' lavender flower spikes via steam distillation. As a lavandin (a natural hybrid of true lavender and spike lavender), its aromatic profile carries a touch more camphor than Lavandula angustifolia hydrosols, alongside the expected linalool and linalyl acetate. As with all hydrosols, natural batch variation in scent and color should be expected. Intended for cosmetic and household use only.

Ingredients

100% pure hydrosol. No fillers, no synthetic fragrance, no preservatives added.
