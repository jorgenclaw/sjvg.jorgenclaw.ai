About this hydrosol

Steam-distilled from Valencia orange peel, this hydrosol carries a true peel-oil bouquet: d-limonene up front, the same aromatic terpene that defines orange essential oil, rounding out into a soft floral finish. Gentler than the essential oil itself, it's well suited to all skin types — especially normal-to-dry skin seeking a light, non-drying toner. Use as a refreshing facial mist, a water substitute in lotions, creams, and clay masks, or as a natural room and linen spray. A cheerful addition to DIY household cleaners, where citrus's natural degreasing properties shine. Pairs well with vanilla, ginger, cinnamon leaf, or ylang ylang for a warmer, richer variation.

Technical note

Distilled from fresh-cut Valencia orange peel at low heat via steam distillation. As with all hydrosols, natural batch variation in scent and color should be expected. Intended for cosmetic and household use only.

Ingredients

100% pure hydrosol. No fillers, no synthetic fragrance, no preservatives added.

Care

Shake well. Keep out of direct sunlight. Refrigeration extends shelf life, but it isn't required.

What is a hydrosol?

A hydrosol is the fragrant water that comes out of a steam still along with a plant's essential oil. It carries the plant's water-soluble aromatic compounds at a much gentler strength than the oil, so it can go straight onto skin, linens, or into recipes without diluting.
