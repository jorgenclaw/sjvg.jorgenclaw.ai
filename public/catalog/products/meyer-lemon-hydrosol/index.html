About this hydrosol

Steam-distilled from Meyer lemon peel, this hydrosol trades true lemon's sharp citral edge for something softer and more floral, a natural result of Meyer lemon's lower acidity and gentler aromatic profile. A gentle, refreshing toner for all skin types, especially oily or combination skin. Mist onto the face after cleansing, use as a water substitute in lotions and creams, or add to a clay mask for a bright citrus lift. Also lovely as a light room or linen spray. Blends well with lavender, rosemary, or a few drops of Meyer lemon essential oil for a deeper citrus note.

Technical note

Distilled from fresh, rinsed Meyer lemon peel via steam distillation. As with all hydrosols, natural batch variation in scent and color should be expected. Intended for cosmetic and household use only.

Ingredients

100% pure hydrosol. No fillers, no synthetic fragrance, no preservatives added.

Care

Shake well. Keep out of direct sunlight. Refrigeration extends shelf life, but it isn't required.

What is a hydrosol?

A hydrosol is the fragrant water that comes out of a steam still along with a plant's essential oil. It carries the plant's water-soluble aromatic compounds at a much gentler strength than the oil, so it can go straight onto skin, linens, or into recipes without diluting.
