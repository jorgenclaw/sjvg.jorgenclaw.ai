About this oil

Distilled from our lemongrass stalk and leaf, this oil is bright, grassy and strongly lemony. It is rich in citral, the same compound behind the sharp top note of lemon peel. It is a favorite for fresh-smelling homemade cleaners and soaps and for outdoor blends. Lemongrass is one of the stronger oils on skin, so it takes more dilution than the others: see the dilution box below.

How much to use
  • Skin: 1 drop per 2 teaspoons of carrier oil, no more
  • Diffuser: 2–3 drops in the water

Never use it undiluted on skin. Can irritate or sensitize skin: on skin, use no more than 1 drop per 2 teaspoons of carrier oil. Patch-test first.

Ingredients

100% pure essential oil. No fillers, no carrier oil, no synthetic fragrance.

Care

Keep the cap tight and the bottle out of heat and direct sunlight. Keep out of reach of children and pets. Essential oils are flammable: keep away from open flame.

What is an essential oil?

An essential oil is the concentrated aromatic oil a plant gives off in a steam still. It floats on top of the hydrosol and is collected separately. It is many times stronger than a hydrosol, so dilute it in a carrier oil before it goes on skin (see How much to use), and never take it by mouth.
