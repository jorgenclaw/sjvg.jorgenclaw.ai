White Sage Hydrosol

Salvia apiana

Description

A soft, herbal, resinous water distilled from white sage — gentler and more wearable than burning the dried leaf, with the same grounding, clarifying character. Use as a room and linen spray, a calming body mist, or a light facial toner for normal-to-oily skin. White sage has long held deep cultural and ceremonial significance for Indigenous peoples of California, and wild populations have faced real pressure from over-harvesting. This hydrosol is distilled entirely from garden-grown, responsibly cultivated California white sage — never wild-harvested — so you can use it with a clear conscience.

Technical Note

Distilled from fresh, garden-grown white sage leaf via steam distillation. As with all hydrosols, natural batch variation in scent and color should be expected. Intended for cosmetic and household use only.

Ingredients

100% pure hydrosol. No fillers, no synthetic fragrance, no preservatives added.
