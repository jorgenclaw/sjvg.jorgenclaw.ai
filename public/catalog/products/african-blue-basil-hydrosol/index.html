About this hydrosol

Steam-distilled from African blue basil leaf and flower, this hydrosol carries a bold, layered bouquet: camphor and 1,8-cineole up front, a spicy eugenol edge reminiscent of clove, and a faint sweet, floral finish. African blue basil is a striking, sterile hybrid between camphor basil and dark opal basil, prized by growers for its purple-veined leaves, lavender flower spikes, and unusually rich fragrance. Bolder and more medicinal than a culinary basil hydrosol, it makes an invigorating room and body spray, a bracing addition to a post-workout mist, or a distinctive note in DIY cleaning sprays. Best appreciated by those who like their herbal scents assertive rather than delicate.

Technical note

Distilled from fresh African blue basil leaf and flower via steam distillation. As a hybrid of camphor basil (Ocimum kilimandscharicum) and basil (Ocimum basilicum), its aromatic profile is naturally higher in camphor and 1,8-cineole than culinary basil varieties. As with all hydrosols, natural batch variation in scent and color should be expected. Intended for cosmetic and household use only; the camphorous character means a patch test is recommended before facial use.

Ingredients

100% pure hydrosol. No fillers, no synthetic fragrance, no preservatives added.

Care

Shake well. Keep out of direct sunlight. Refrigeration extends shelf life, but it isn't required.

What is a hydrosol?

A hydrosol is the fragrant water that comes out of a steam still along with a plant's essential oil. It carries the plant's water-soluble aromatic compounds at a much gentler strength than the oil, so it can go straight onto skin, linens, or into recipes without diluting.
