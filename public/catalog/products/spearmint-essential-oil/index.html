About this oil

Distilled from our spearmint leaf and stem, this oil smells like fresh spearmint gum: sweet and minty, built on carvone with limonene in support. Spearmint holds very little menthol, so it is softer and less sharp than peppermint. It brightens a room in a diffuser, lifts a homemade soap or lip balm, and blends easily with lavender and lemongrass.

How much to use
  • Face: 3 drops per tablespoon of carrier oil (1%)
  • Body: 6 drops per tablespoon of carrier oil (2%)
  • Diffuser: 3–5 drops in the water

Never use it undiluted on skin. Keep away from the faces of babies and young children. Can irritate sensitive skin, so dilute well.

Ingredients

100% pure essential oil. No fillers, no carrier oil, no synthetic fragrance.

Care

Keep the cap tight and the bottle out of heat and direct sunlight. Keep out of reach of children and pets. Essential oils are flammable: keep away from open flame.

What is an essential oil?

An essential oil is the concentrated aromatic oil a plant gives off in a steam still. It floats on top of the hydrosol and is collected separately. It is many times stronger than a hydrosol, so dilute it in a carrier oil before it goes on skin (see How much to use), and never take it by mouth.
